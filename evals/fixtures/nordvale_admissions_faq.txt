NORDVALE UNIVERSITY - ADMISSIONS FAQ (SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT)

This document is fictional and contains no real contact details or personal
data. The addresses below use the reserved .example domain.

Q: What is the minimum grade point average?
A: The minimum GPA for consideration is 3.2 on a four point scale.

Q: When are interviews held?
A: Interviews are held in the second week of April. All interviews are online;
Nordvale does not hold in-person interviews.

Q: How do I contact the admissions office?
A: Write to admissions@nordvale.example. The office replies within five
working days.

Q: When are decisions released?
A: Decisions are released 30 days after the interview.

Q: Can I defer my place?
A: A place may be deferred once, for up to one year. A second deferral is not
granted under any circumstances.

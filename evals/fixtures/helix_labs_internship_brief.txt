HELIX LABS - SUMMER RESIDENCY BRIEF (SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT)

Helix Labs is a fictional organisation invented for evaluation purposes.

Duration
The Helix Labs Summer Residency runs for 12 weeks, from June to August.

Stipend
Residents receive a monthly stipend of 2,100 credits. The stipend is paid on
the last working day of each month.

Application statement
Applicants submit a written statement. The Helix Labs statement limit is
200 words shorter than the personal statement limit set by the Nordvale
Merit Scholarship policy, so applicants who have written a Nordvale statement
must shorten it before submitting.

Review
Applications are reviewed by the Residency Panel. The panel does not conduct
interviews.

Mentoring
Every resident is assigned a mentor in week 2 of the residency.

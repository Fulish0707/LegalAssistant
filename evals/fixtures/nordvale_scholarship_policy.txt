NORDVALE UNIVERSITY - MERIT SCHOLARSHIP POLICY (SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT)

This document is fictional. Nordvale University does not exist and no part of
this file describes a real person, institution or award.

1. Award
The Nordvale Merit Scholarship awards 4,500 credits per academic year. The
award is renewable for a second year on satisfactory academic progress.

2. Deadline
Applications close on 14 March. Late applications are not reviewed and are not
carried over to the following cycle.

3. Personal statement
Each applicant submits one personal statement of no more than 800 words. The
statement must describe the applicant's motivation and one project they led.

4. References
Applicants must submit two reference letters. At least one letter must come
from an academic referee.

5. Committee
The scholarship committee is chaired by Dr. Imelda Farrow. The committee meets
once per cycle and its decisions are final.
